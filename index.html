Better Skyward loading…

A redesigned Skyward. A home screen with what's happening now, grades with every assignment, your schedule as a timeline, attendance, and the rest of Skyward's menu, all in one app. It reads the Skyward you're already logged in to. Your grades only ever go between your browser and Skyward.

iPhone · iPad · Safari · no apps

1. Make the Better Skyward bookmark (once)

  2. Tap Share ⬆︎ → Add to Favorites. Name it Better Skyward → Save.
  3. Open your bookmarks: tap the 📖 button (on newer iPhones, ⋯ → Bookmarks), open Favorites, tap Edit, then tap Better Skyward.
  4. Tap the address under the name, delete it all, paste, then tap Done.

2. Every time

  1. Tap Better Skyward on your start page. It opens the Skyward sign-in page.
  2. Sign in.
  3. Tap the address bar, type Better, and tap ☆ Better Skyward under Favorites or Bookmarks. Better Skyward opens over Skyward.

Tapping it from the address bar is what makes it run on the Skyward page you're on. If tapping it on your start page doesn't open Skyward, use Open Skyward and add that page to your Favorites too.

Android · Chrome · no apps

1. Make the Better Skyward bookmark (once)

  2. Tap ⋮ → ☆ to bookmark this page.
  3. Tap ⋮ → Bookmarks → ⋮ next to it → Edit. Name it Better Skyward, clear the URL, paste, and go back.

2. Every time

  1. Tap the address bar, type Better, and tap ☆ Better Skyward. It opens the Skyward sign-in page.
  2. Sign in.
  3. Tap the address bar again, type Better, and tap ☆ Better Skyward. Better Skyward opens over Skyward.

Chrome only runs it when you pick it from the address bar, not from the Bookmarks list.

Computer · no installs

1. Save the bookmark (once)

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl + Shift + B).
  2. Drag this button onto the bar: ✦ Better Skyward

2. Every time

  1. Click ✦ Better Skyward. It opens the Skyward sign-in page.
  2. Sign in, then click ✦ Better Skyward again.

Updating? Delete the old bookmark and drag the new one. The app tells you when a new version is out.

Other way

Paste it into the console

  1. Sign in to Skyward.
  2. Press F12 (or Ctrl + Shift + J) and open the Console tab.
  3. Paste the code and press Enter. Chrome may ask you to type allow pasting first.
