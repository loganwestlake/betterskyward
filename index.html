Skyward Glow

Your Skyward grades on one clean dashboard: grade rings, term tabs, a GPA estimate, and which grades changed since you last checked. It reads the Skyward page you already have open. Nothing gets sent anywhere.

Option A · easiest

Save it as a bookmark

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl + Shift + B).
  2. Drag this button onto the bar:
✦ Skyward Glow
  1. Log in to Skyward, open Gradebook, then click the bookmark.
Option B

Paste it into the console

  1. Open Skyward → Gradebook.
  2. Press F12 (or Ctrl + Shift + J) and open the Console tab.
  3. Paste the code and press Enter. Chrome may ask you to type allow pasting first.
Try it first

Practice on a fake gradebook

Open the practice page, which has made-up grades, and test Option A or B there before trying it on real Skyward.
